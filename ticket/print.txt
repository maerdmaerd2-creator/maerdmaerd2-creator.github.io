ECHO HOLLOW / TWO-SIDED PRINT TICKET

PAPER EDITION
The ticket page has an illustrated front and reverse for paper use.
The STL components are simpler raised reliefs, not exact sculpted copies of the illustration.

TWO PARTS
- ticket/echo_hollow_front.stl (station name)
- ticket/echo_hollow_dot_reverse.stl (Dot by the pond)

Both models are 74 x 42 mm and around 2.23 mm high.
Print each flat face-up, detailed face pointing away from the bed; join the flat backs.

For an initial PLA FDM test with a 0.4 mm nozzle, try 0.16-0.20 mm layers,
2-3 walls, 15-25% infill. Inspect the actual sliced preview before printing.
The smaller lettering and sleeping-cat lines deserve a close look.

The ticket carries no electronic sound component. The separate QR card opens
its online birdsong when scanned by a phone with internet access:
https://maerdmaerd2-creator.github.io/ticket/
Print the QR only after checking that link works on your phone.

The models are supplied as STL geometry. The editable generator model.py
uses CadQuery, Shapely and trimesh. Printer-specific G-code depends on
printer/nozzle/material and must be created in a suitable slicer.

— MAERD
