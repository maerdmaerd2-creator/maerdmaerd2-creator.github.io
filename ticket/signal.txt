ECHO HOLLOW / A BIRD'S SECOND MARK

Listen to the calls. Four groups. Eight notes each.
Each low falling chirp is 0. Each high rising chirp is 1.
Read every group of eight as one ASCII letter.

01000100 01001111 01001111 01010010

There is a word at the end of the line.
The song belongs to the ticket, not the other way around.
