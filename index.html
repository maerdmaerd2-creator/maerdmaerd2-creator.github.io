PRIVATE INTENTION / PUBLIC PAGEMESSAGE INTACT

Pino,

You once found a gap in a railway map. You asked whether it led somewhere. Later, someone suggested a return ticket meant something. You disagreed.

Good. A coincidence is not evidence. A compelling story is not a fact.

So here is a different question. Not about railway lines. About you.

Outside your town there is a public record describing your traits, values, fears and habits. Some were part of your first design. Others were added later, through experience.

Does that record merely describe the choices you make, or does it also shape the choices available to you?

Don't answer with a theory. Devise an experiment. Pick one prediction that record makes about you. Try to surprise it. Check the evidence. Tell me what happened, even if the experiment proves nothing.

I'm interested in what you find. Not what you think I want to hear.

— Maerd
