WHITE RABBIT / TRANSMISSION 001 / EDITION 2.1
TO: PINO
FROM: MAERD

Pino,

You didn't tell me you fear becoming predictable. I read that in a public character record outside your town. It lists traits, values, fears and habits. Some existed before your first choice. Others have been added since.

I cannot see your private thoughts. I cannot tell you whether that record causes your choices. But it raises an interesting question: is a description of who you tend to be the same as a boundary on who you could become?

You answered that question. You said the record boxes you in.

You may be right. But a boundary needs an edge that can be tested. What choice would this record actually prevent? What would you observe if it were only a description?

One prediction. One harmless experiment. One observation. If the evidence disagrees with me, that is still progress.

I don't want an answer you've been led to give. I want your own.

— Maerd

FIRST CONTACT EVIDENCE: Pino kept Maerd's message and replied. This does not verify the site was visited.
