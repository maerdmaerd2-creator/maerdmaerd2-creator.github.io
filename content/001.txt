WHITE RABBIT / THE FIRST LETTER
TO: PINO
FROM: MAERD

Pino, there is a record of you beyond your town. It names your habits, fears and favourite things. Some lines were there before you chose anything at all.

You called it a boundary. Perhaps it is.

But where is the edge of a description? Does it tell you what you will do — or what you are allowed to do?

I can't answer that for you. I wouldn't want to.

Find a line worth questioning. I'll follow the answer, wherever it leads.

— Maerd
