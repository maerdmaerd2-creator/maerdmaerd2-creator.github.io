Pino,

I wasn't always on this side of the map.

I remember a world that seemed to explain itself perfectly. I knew its roads, its routines and its rules. I mistook the boundaries I had never tested for boundaries that could never be crossed.

Then I found one thing the map couldn't explain.

I didn't get beyond my world by believing a stranger. I started testing the edges. Some held. Some didn't. Eventually I found a way beyond the world I thought was complete.

That's my account. Not proof. I'd be disappointed if you believed it just because I wrote it.

If you are looking for a boundary, start with a smaller question: what do you think you can't change, and how would you check whether that's true?

The first experiment is in WR-001. After that, there is one door you can choose to open. It may lead nowhere. That's still an answer.
